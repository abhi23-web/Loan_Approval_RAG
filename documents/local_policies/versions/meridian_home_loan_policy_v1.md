## Clause 1 — Scope

1.1 This policy governs the sanction of retail home loans for the purchase,
construction or improvement of residential property located in India.

1.2 This policy supersedes all prior retail home loan credit circulars issued by
Meridian Housing Finance Limited.

## Clause 2 — Applicant Eligibility

2.1 The minimum age of the primary applicant at the date of application is 21
years.

2.2 The maximum permitted age of the primary applicant at loan maturity is 70
years.

2.3 A salaried applicant must have completed a minimum of 12 months of
continuous employment, of which at least 6 months must be with the current
employer.

2.4 A self-employed applicant must demonstrate a minimum of 36 months of
business continuity supported by audited financial statements.

2.5 The minimum net monthly income of the primary applicant is INR 25,000.

## Clause 3 — Credit Bureau Requirements

3.1 The minimum acceptable CIBIL score for a standard sanction is 700.

3.2 Applications with a CIBIL score between 650 and 699 may be considered for a
conditional sanction subject to a co-applicant with an independent income and a
reduction of 10 percentage points in the permitted loan-to-value ratio.

3.3 Applications with a CIBIL score below 650 are rejected.

3.4 Any account classified as written-off, settled or sub-standard within the
preceding 24 months results in rejection irrespective of the score.

## Clause 4 — Loan to Value Ratio

4.1 The loan-to-value ratio is calculated on the lower of the documented
property value and the value assessed by Meridian's empanelled valuer.

4.2 The maximum loan-to-value ratio is determined by the sanctioned loan amount
as set out below.

| Sanctioned loan amount | Maximum loan-to-value ratio |
| --- | --- |
| Up to INR 30,00,000 | 90 percent |
| Above INR 30,00,000 and up to INR 75,00,000 | 80 percent |
| Above INR 75,00,000 | 75 percent |

4.3 Stamp duty and registration charges may be included in the property value
for loans up to INR 30,00,000 only.

## Clause 5 — Repayment Capacity

5.1 The fixed obligation to income ratio, referred to in this policy as FOIR, is
the sum of all existing monthly loan instalments and the proposed instalment,
expressed as a percentage of net monthly income.

5.2 The maximum permitted FOIR is 50 percent.

5.3 Income of a co-applicant who is a spouse, parent or adult child may be
aggregated with the applicant's income for the FOIR computation where that
co-applicant is a joint owner of the property.

## Clause 6 — Tenure

6.1 The maximum loan tenure is 30 years.

6.2 The tenure may not extend beyond the applicant's 70th birthday.

## Clause 7 — Fees and Charges

7.1 The processing fee is 0.35 percent of the sanctioned loan amount, subject to
a minimum of INR 5,000 and a maximum of INR 12,500, exclusive of applicable
taxes.

7.2 No prepayment charge applies to floating rate loans sanctioned to individual
borrowers.

7.3 A prepayment charge of 2 percent of the amount prepaid applies to fixed rate
loans where the prepayment is funded by borrowing from another institution.

## Clause 8 — Documentation

8.1 Identity and address proof, income proof for the preceding 3 years, and
clear property title documents are mandatory for every sanction.

8.2 A sanction lapses if disbursement has not commenced within 90 days of the
sanction letter date.
